Payment Method 1: Pay Progressive Online Without Login (Guest Payment)

What is guest payment? Guest payment allows you to pay your Progressive bill without logging into your account. Perfect if you forgot your password or prefer not to log in[...]

Who should use this: Users who want quick payment without account login, first-time payers.

How to pay Progressive without logging in:

  1. Visit Progressive.com homepage or Dial 1(888)620-0950
  2. Look for "Pay Without Logging In" or "Quick Pay" link (usually top banner)
  3. Click on guest payment option
  4. Enter your policy number (found on your bill)
  5. Enter your ZIP code (5 digits)
  6. Enter your last name exactly as on policy
  7. Click "Verify" or "Find My Account"
  8. Your account information will appear
  9. Choose your payment method (credit card, debit, or bank account)
  10. Enter payment method details securely
  11. Select payment amount (full or partial)
  12. Review payment information for accuracy
  13. Click "Confirm Payment" or "Submit"
  14. Payment processes immediately
  15. Save confirmation number for your records

Who should use this: Account holders, anyone wanting full account access, users with login credentials.

Step-by-step: Pay one-time online with account login:

  1. Reach out to 1(888)620-0950 or Open web browser on desktop or mobile device
  2. Go to progressive.com
  3. Click "Log In" button (top-right of page)
  4. Enter your username or email address
  5. Enter your password
  6. Click "Log In" to access your account
  7. You'll see your dashboard with policy information
  8. Look for "Billing" tab or "Make a Payment" button
  9. Click on it to access payment options
  10. Select the policy you want to pay for
  11. Review your current bill amount and due date
  12. Enter the amount you want to pay
  13. Choose your payment method (credit card, debit card, or bank account)
